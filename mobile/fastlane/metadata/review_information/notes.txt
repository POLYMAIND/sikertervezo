2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Sikermaker is a daily planner for small business owners and independent sales people in Hungary (Hungarian UI). It replaces paper planners and scattered notes: users plan their 5 daily steps, tasks and appointments, track habits and monthly goals, keep follow-ups with contacts and clients, plan social media content, and close each day with reflection questions. The value: one place for the daily routine that drives their business, with morning/evening reminders, synced between iPhone and the web (sikermaker.hu).

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE FEATURES
Demo account (all features enabled): see Sign-In Information. Sign in on the first screen ("Belépés" tab). Without an account you can tap "Kipróbálom belépés nélkül" to try the app with sample data.
Main areas (bottom tab bar, "Több" = More): Vezérlőpult (dashboard), Naptár (calendar), Szokások (habits), Gondolattérkép (mind map), Olvasás (reading), Kapcsolatok (contacts), Ügyfelek (clients), Tartalom (content plan), Beállítások (settings). Reminders: Beállítások → Emlékeztetők (local notifications). Account deletion: Beállítások → Fiók → "Fiókom törlése" (type your email to confirm).
Paid content: Kapcsolatok (contacts pipeline), Ügyfelek (clients and orders), Tartalom (content calendar) and calendar sync belong to the "Pro" plan. Pro is sold only on our website as a multiplatform subscription (Guideline 3.1.3(b)); the app contains no purchase option, prices, or links to buy. Users who already have Pro on their account see these features after signing in; free users do not see them in the app at all. The demo account has Pro enabled.
User-generated content: all content is private to the user's own account. There is no sharing, feed, chat or any content visible to other users, so no reporting/blocking is needed.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (EU region): authentication and database (the user's plan data).
- Vercel: hosting of our API (sikermaker.hu/api).
- Brevo: transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset).
- Stripe and Számlázz.hu: payment and invoicing for the Pro plan on our website only (not used in the app).
- Optional, user-initiated: PolyOS (our own business software) account linking; calendar feeds (iCal).
No AI services, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no tracking.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions. The interface and content are in Hungarian.
